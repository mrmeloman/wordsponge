# Changelog

## 1.3

- Works in current Chrome (121 or newer) and in Firefox and Firefox-based browsers such as Zen (140 or newer).
- Fully local by default: nothing is downloaded from thewordsponge.com unless you turn it on in settings.
- Add your own words by typing them in settings or uploading a .txt, .csv, .tsv or .json file. They survive daily list updates.
- Choose how often words are replaced: Light, Balanced, Intense, Everything, or your own limits per word and per page. A quick switch is in the popup.
- Words with accents and non-Latin scripts are matched correctly, and large word lists stay fast.
- Translated text no longer breaks page layouts, SVG charts or sites that reload content as you browse.
