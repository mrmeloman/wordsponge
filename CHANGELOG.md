# Changelog

## 1.4

- Set how often each word appears: Focus for new or important words (shown more often and picked first), Normal, Familiar (shown rarely), or Known (never shown, kept in your list). A word can also have its own limit per page.
- Click a translated word to see the original and set its level right on the page.
- Your words now have a searchable table in settings, with a level and limit per word and a way to set many words at once. Words from the downloaded list can have levels too.
- Pause Word Sponge on the current page from the toolbar button, the word card, or the keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+P. Replacements disappear at once and come back when you reload or return. A notice lets you resume or always skip the page.
- Turn Word Sponge off for a single page, not only a whole site. Settings lists every page and site where it is off, with a way to turn each back on.

## 1.3

- Works in current Chrome (121 or newer) and in Firefox and Firefox-based browsers such as Zen (140 or newer).
- Fully local by default: nothing is downloaded from thewordsponge.com unless you turn it on in settings.
- Add your own words by typing them in settings or uploading a .txt, .csv, .tsv or .json file. They survive daily list updates.
- Choose how often words are replaced: Light, Balanced, Intense, Everything, or your own limits per word and per page. A quick switch is in the popup.
- Words with accents and non-Latin scripts are matched correctly, and large word lists stay fast.
- Translated text no longer breaks page layouts, SVG charts or sites that reload content as you browse.
